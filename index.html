Who We Are

Women Owned.
Women Led.

Gabriela Flores spent years renovating homes across Manhattan, managing co-op boards, NYC Department of Buildings filings and demanding building schedules. Originally from Peru and now based in Glen Ridge, she brings that same precision to homes in Glen Ridge, Montclair and across Essex County.

Her commitment to quality craftsmanship, thoughtful design and reliable execution has earned the trust of clients, architects and colleagues, and a business built on referrals and repeat clients.
